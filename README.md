# wordle-hints-today

A companion resource for Wordle players: practical strategy guides, solving tips, and a daily pointer to the day's hint — so you can sharpen your guesses instead of just guessing.

## How to use daily hints

A good hint narrows the field without handing you the answer. Here is how to make the most of them:

1. **Start with a strong opening word.** Words that cover the most common letters — like CRANE, SLATE, TRACE, or ADIEU — eliminate the most possibilities. A consonant-heavy opener (CRANE, STARE) followed by a vowel-heavy second guess (ADIEU) is a classic one-two punch.

2. **Think in letter frequency, not just favorites.** In English, E, A, R, O, T, L, I, S, and N show up far more often in Wordle answers than Q, Z, J, or X. After your opener, prioritize guesses built from untested high-frequency letters.

3. **Use yellows to pin down position.** A yellow letter is in the word — it just is not in *that* spot. Move it to a new slot each guess, and resist re-playing gray letters. Two or three confirmed letters in fresh positions usually crack the puzzle.

4. **Hard Mode is the best teacher.** Turning on Hard Mode forces every guess to use your revealed hints, which trains you to plan two moves ahead instead of burning guesses. Even one Hard Mode puzzle a week sharpens your instincts noticeably.
